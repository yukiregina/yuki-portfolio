About

I grew up between two cultures that don't naturally speak to each other. That gap became the job: finding what's missing before it reaches the screen.

Tokyo trained me as a graphic designer. São Paulo turned me into a product designer: advertising, art direction, then B2B products. Before any of that, I changed schools every two years. Reading a room before I could speak its language was a survival skill. In product discovery, they pay you for it.

What I keep finding is the same thing in different products: the real problem sitting between teams, where nobody was looking. The projects above carry the evidence.

When the design is right, the business numbers move. Open to remote, global roles. Open to client projects. If what you need is the deck to look nicer, I'll be terrible for you.
